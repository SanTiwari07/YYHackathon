## 1. Weighted Evaluation Rubric

The judging panel utilizes a 5-dimension rubric totaling 100 points during Phase 1 and Phase 2 evaluations:

| Pillar | Weight | Key Evaluation Focus | Common Failure Modes to Avoid |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| **Impact & Measurability** | **25%** | • Rigor and magnitude of quantified savings.<br>• Grounding against official baseline datasets.<br>• Clear formulas with explicit units.<br>• Credibility of smallholder yield uplift. | • Claiming unverified percentage claims ("saves 90% water").<br>• Omitting baseline data sources.<br>• Inability to show mathematical derivation. |
| **Problem Understanding & Idea Quality** | **20%** | • Deep empathy with Indian smallholder context.<br>• Understanding erratic 3-phase grid power and pump burnouts.<br>• Originality beyond standard IoT dashboards. | • Assuming continuous 24/7 high-speed 4G connectivity.<br>• Ignoring land fragmentation and free power subsidies.<br>• Generic "AI chatbot" without physical actuation. |
| **Architecture & Design** | **20%** | • Soundness of end-to-end multi-layer architecture.<br>• Explicit data, energy, and financial flow paths.<br>• Coherent integration of sensing, edge, and cloud. | • Monolithic architectures without offline resilience.<br>• Ignoring hardware/software interface realities.<br>• Disconnected mockups that lack functional flow. |
| **Feasibility & Affordability** | **20%** | • Total system capex within reach of smallholders.<br>• Payback period (<2 seasons / <18 months).<br>• Ruggedized physical durability (IP65, heat/dust/lightning). | • Proposing ₹30,000+ IoT kits for 1-acre subsistence plots.<br>• Complex maintenance requiring skilled technicians.<br>• High recurring cloud API fees passed to the farmer. |
| **Sustainability** | **15%** | • Lifecycle energy, water, and GHG emissions impact.<br>• Aquifer recharge and groundwater conservation.<br>• Synergies with circular economy & renewable energy. | • Rebound effect (cheap solar pump causing more water extraction).<br>• Ignoring battery/e-waste lifecycle. |

## 2. Winning Formula & Strategic Checklist

To score in the top 1% (Shortlist for Bengaluru Finale and PPI):

1. **Address the Rebound Effect Directly:** When farmers receive subsidized solar pumps under PM-KUSUM, electricity becomes free during daytime hours. Without automated demand limits, farmers over-pump groundwater at zero marginal cost, accelerating aquifer depletion! A winning solution MUST show how its edge intelligence couples solar pumping with scientific water quotas, diverting surplus power to cold storage or the grid rather than over-extracting water.
2. **Double Down on Schneider Electric Ecosystem:** Frame the architecture naturally as an extension of Schneider’s **EcoStruxure for Agriculture & Water**, incorporating Altivar™ solar VFD concepts, TeSys™ motor protections, and EcoStruxure Microgrid Advisor paradigms.
3. **Deliver a Live, Interactive Simulation:** Do not rely on static PowerPoint slides. Present an interactive web console that simulates live solar insolation variations, pump motor frequency adjustments, soil moisture dynamics, and power redirection in real time.
