## 2. Graphic Treatment & Rendering Standards
- **Aspect Ratio:** Fixed 16:9 widescreen canvas (1920 × 1080 px).
- **Surface Elevation:** Dark industrial UI theme (`#0B1115` canvas, `#151A1C` cards, `#1D2428` panels) with Schneider Green (`#3DCD58`) and Solar Gold (`#FFCE00`) accents.
- **Image Treatment:** Subtle radial vignette and gradient dark fade overlays (`rgba(11, 17, 21, 0.85)`) to maintain strict WCAG AA+ contrast (minimum 7:1 for body copy, 4.5:1 for large metrics).
