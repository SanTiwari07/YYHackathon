## 1. Corporate Profile & Purpose

* **Global Overview:** Schneider Electric SE is a European multinational company (Euronext: SU, CAC 40 index) founded in 1836. Headquartered in Rueil-Malmaison, France, it is recognized globally as the world’s most sustainable corporation (Corporate Knights Global 100) and a leader in digital transformation of energy management and automation.
* **Core Purpose Statement:** *"Our mission is to be your digital partner for Sustainability and Efficiency."*
* **Key Operating Statistics:**
  - Operations in 100+ countries.
  - ~150,000+ global employees.
  - €36+ Billion annual global revenue.
  - ~5.5% of annual revenue reinvested in R&D.

## 2. Strategic Pillars: "Electricity 4.0"

Schneider Electric frames the global energy transition through the concept of **"Electricity 4.0"** — the convergence of:
1. **Electric:** Electricity is the most efficient form of energy and the primary vector for decarbonization. Electrifying heating, transport, and agriculture can cut energy waste by up to 50%.
2. **Digital:** Digitalization (IoT, big data, AI, digital twins) makes invisible energy waste visible and manageable, allowing real-time optimization of consumption, storage, and generation.

## 3. Schneider Electric India Footprint & Strategic Priority

India is central to Schneider Electric’s global strategy:
* **Market Scale:** India is Schneider Electric’s **3rd largest market globally** by revenue and employee headcount.
* **Global Hub:** India is one of Schneider's **four global innovation and R&D hubs** (alongside France, the US, and China).
* **Engineering Footprint in India:**
  - **Global R&D Hub:** Major innovation centers in Bengaluru and Hyderabad employing thousands of engineers.
  - **Manufacturing Network:** Over **30 manufacturing plants** across India (including Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Vadodara, and Ahmednagar).
  - **Smart Factories:** Schneider’s Hyderabad and Bengaluru plants are recognized by the World Economic Forum (WEF) as Global Lighthouse Smart Factories.
* **L&T Electrical & Automation (E&A) Integration:** Schneider Electric completed the landmark ₹14,000 Crore acquisition of Larsen & Toubro's Electrical & Automation business in India, giving Schneider unmatched grassroots rural, industrial, and agricultural market penetration across the Indian subcontinent.

## 4. SE Ventures: Strategic Capital Mandate

* **Fund Profile:** SE Ventures is Schneider Electric’s corporate venture capital arm with over €1 Billion in assets under management (AUM).
* **Investment Philosophy:** Operates independently to back visionary entrepreneurs solving climate tech, clean energy, industrial automation, cybersecurity, and mobility challenges.
* **India / APAC Leadership:** Spearheaded by Siddharth Mehta (Partner, SE Ventures), actively investing in early-stage Indian energy and hardware-tech enablers.
* **Notable India Footprint Portfolio Companies:**
  - **Aerem:** Rooftop solar tech and financing platform for Indian commercial/industrial SMEs.
  - **Elecbits:** AI-driven electronic hardware design and contract manufacturing platform.
  - **AiDash:** Satellite-first AI platform for critical infrastructure and grid vegetation management.
* **What SE Ventures Looks For:**
  1. High capital efficiency with massive addressable market in emerging economies.
  2. Proprietary software/edge algorithms that unlock real hardware utility.
  3. Clear unit economics without reliance on permanent state subsidies.
