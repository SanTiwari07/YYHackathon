## 2. Indian Agri-Tech Innovation Waves

India has witnessed three distinct waves of agricultural technology innovation over the last 15 years:

### Wave 1 (2010–2017): Remote Connectivity & Mobile Starters
* **Core Product:** 2G GSM motor controllers (e.g., Kisan Raja, Mobitech, Nano Ganesh).
* **Value Delivered:** Allowed farmers to turn pumps ON/OFF via mobile call/SMS, saving dangerous nighttime trips to fields.
* **Fundamental Limitation:** Zero agronomic intelligence. Farmers still turned pumps on and left them running blindly, continuing the exhaustion of groundwater and wasting subsidized electricity.

### Wave 2 (2017–2022): Enterprise SaaS & Supply Chain AgTech
* **Core Product:** Satellite crop monitoring and farm management ERPs (e.g., Cropin, DeHaat, Ninjacart).
* **Value Delivered:** Digitized farm boundaries, predicted regional crop yields, and streamlined commodity trading for input companies and banks.
* **Fundamental Limitation:** B2B enterprise focus. Marginal benefit to the individual smallholder standing in a parched field with a failing borewell; no direct physical control over field equipment.

### Wave 3 (2022–Present): Deep-Tech IoT & Renewable Integration
* **Core Product:** AI microclimate hardware (Fasal), solar pump VFDs (Ecozen, Shakti Pumps), and farm-gate solar cold rooms (Ecofrost).
* **Current Bottleneck:** Fragmented silos. The solar pump controller does not communicate with the soil moisture sensor, and neither communicates with the farm-gate cold room. As a result, solar power is wasted when pumps are idle, and groundwater is depleted when pumps run unconstrained.
