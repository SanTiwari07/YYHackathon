## Slide 03: The Solar Rebound Paradox & Systemic Gap

- **Slide Number:** 03
- **Slide Title:** The Solar Rebound Paradox: Why Existing Solutions Fail
- **Subtitle:** PM-KUSUM solved clean energy access, but zero marginal cost has created a dangerous perverse incentive.
- **Objective:** Explain why giving farmers free solar electricity causes aquifer over-extraction (Jevons paradox) and wastes 63% of generated power.
- **Audience Takeaway:** Telling a farmer to stop pumping does nothing because stopping has zero economic return. Without a valuable alternative destination for solar energy, solar pumps accelerate water depletion.
- **Visual Structure & Layout:**
  - *Left (50% width):* The Rebound Mechanism Diagram. Econometric evidence from Gupta (2019, *Energy Policy*): Standalone solar pumps lead to **+16% to 39% increase in groundwater extraction** because marginal cost per hour of pumping is ₹0.
  - *Right (50% width):* The Wasted Asset Reality. Bar chart visual comparing solar generation vs pump consumption. Shah et al. (IWMI) benchmark confirmed: Out of 6,559 kWh generated by a 4.8 kWp pump array, only 2,422 kWh is needed for irrigation $\to$ **4,137 kWh/year (63%) sits completely idle**.
  - *Bottom Card:* The Industry Gap. "Commercial cold rooms (Ecozen, etc.) force farmers to buy a second redundant 4 kWp PV array (₹91,000 wasted capex). IoT advisory apps (Fasal) provide read-only graphs with zero actuation."
- **Design Tokens:** Solar Gold (`#FFCE00`), Transient Orange (`#E47F00`), JetBrains Mono for data readouts.
- **Verified Data Used:** 10.9 lakh PM-KUSUM pumps, 16–39% extraction increase, 6,559 kWh generated, 4,137 kWh idle (63.1%), ₹91,000 redundant PV capex.

- **Subtitle:** From sunrise irrigation to afternoon pre-cooling: a deterministic, automated 24-hour cycle.
- **Objective:** Demonstrate the automated daily sequence, soil moisture dynamics, and fail-safe water shutoff.
- **Audience Takeaway:** Water delivery stops automatically when root-zone field capacity (45%) is satisfied or daily quota is exhausted. Anti-water hammer sequencing prevents pipe rupture.
- **Visual Structure & Layout:**
  - *Top Bar:* Diurnal Timeline Scrubber (06:00 to 18:00) showing Irradiance curve, Position 1 (Irrigation: 08:30–11:30 AM), 5-Second Deadband Dwell (11:30 AM), Position 2 (Pre-Cooling: 11:30 AM–03:30 PM), Overnight PCM Hold.
  - *Left Graphic (40% width):* Physical Soil Depth Column showing 10cm surface & 30cm root-zone capacitive FDR probes, highlighting Wilting Point ($18\%$) $\to$ Current Level ($38\%$) $\to$ Field Capacity Cutoff ($45\%$).
  - *Center Graphic (35% width):* 4-Step Anti-Water Hammer Sequence:
    1. Quota or Saturation Triggered $\to$ 2. VFD ramps to 0 Hz (8s) $\to$ 3. Latching Solenoid Closes at Zero Flow $\to$ 4. 5s DC Bus Deadband.
