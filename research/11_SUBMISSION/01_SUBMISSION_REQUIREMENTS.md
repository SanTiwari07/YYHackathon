## 2. Formatting & Presentation Guidelines

1. **Clear Structural Headings:** Use bold demarcations and bullet points to enable rapid scanning by busy technical evaluators.
2. **Explicit Citations:** Every quantitative claim must include its official institutional source (e.g., *“According to Central Ground Water Board (CGWB) 2023 report...”*).
3. **Diagram Embedding:** Convert all Mermaid architecture flowcharts into high-resolution, vector-rendered SVG/PNG graphics embedded in the final PDF submission.
4. **Interactive Hyperlink:** Provide a direct, clickable URL to the live hosted interactive web prototype and demonstration video.
