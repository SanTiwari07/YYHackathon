## 2. Speaker Delivery Notes & Jury Anticipation

* **Key Psychological Triggers for Schneider Judges:**
  - Emphasize that we are **not** trying to reinvent the motor or the VFD. We treat the **Altivar ATV320** as the best-in-class power heart and build the digital brain that unlocks its full potential.
  - Stress the **Rebound Effect**. Most competitors will present naive solar pump systems that just run all day. Pointing out that free solar power accelerates groundwater collapse without closed-loop water budgeting immediately elevates our team to domain experts.
  - Highlight that our BOM is **under ₹3,500**. This demonstrates profound empathy with Indian rural economics and proves our solution is ready for immediate real-world deployment.
